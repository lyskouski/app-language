# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-17

Thank you for using Tlum, a language learning application developed by the terCAD team (the
"App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data and protects your privacy.
We are committed to protecting your personal information and to being transparent about what
the App does with it.

## Data Collection and Usage

The App does not collect any personal information or sensitive data, and we do not run any
servers that receive your data. You do not need to create an account, sign up or provide any
personal details to use the App's features.

The App does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it does not use cookies.

## Microphone and Voice Recordings

The pronunciation practice ("Articulation") feature uses your device's microphone to record
your voice, but only after you press the Record button. Recording stops automatically after
at most 15 seconds.

Recordings are processed entirely on your device to compare your pronunciation with a
reference sample. They are never uploaded or shared. Temporary recordings are deleted
automatically the next time the App starts.

You can deny or revoke microphone access at any time in your device settings. Only the
pronunciation practice feature will be unavailable.

## Text-to-Speech (Google Translate)

If a word or phrase in your vocabulary has no bundled audio file, the App can generate its
pronunciation using the text-to-speech service of Google Translate. In that case, the App
sends **only the word or phrase and its language code** to Google (`translate.google.com`).
The audio received is stored on your device so the same request is not repeated.

As with any internet request, Google receives technical information such as your device's IP
address. This data is processed by Google under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
No other information from the App is sent with these requests. If you prefer not to use this
service, keep your device offline while studying or use vocabulary that already includes
audio files.

## Data Storage and Security

All data created while using the App, such as your vocabulary, categories, learning progress,
settings and generated audio, is stored locally on your device in the App's private storage.
Learning progress used to personalise your study sets is calculated on your device.

You can export a language pair to a file and import it again, for example to transfer your
vocabulary between devices. Exported files are saved only to the location you choose, and
you control who has access to them.

You can delete your data at any time by removing words, categories or language pairs inside
the App, by clearing the App's data in your device settings, or by uninstalling the App.

## Device Permissions

The App requests only the permissions it needs to work:

- **Microphone**: to record your voice for pronunciation practice (see above).
- **Internet**: to generate pronunciation audio (see above).
- **Storage** (older Android versions only): to save and open the import/export files you choose.

The App does not use your camera, location, contacts or any other personal data on your device.

## Third-Party Services

Apart from the text-to-speech service described above, the App does not send data to third
parties. The App may contain links to external pages or services that are not operated by us.
We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you access
through the App.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under
the age of 13. Because all data stays on the device, no personal information of children is
transferred to us.

## Updates to the Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be
published together with a new version of the App and reflected in the effective date above.
By continuing to use the App after any changes to this Privacy Policy, you agree to be bound
by the updated terms.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the App's
data practices, please contact us at [support@tercad.pl](mailto:support@tercad.pl).

## Conclusion

Your privacy is important to us. The App keeps your data on your device, records your voice
only when you choose to, and contacts an external service only to generate pronunciation
audio for the words you study. By using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.
